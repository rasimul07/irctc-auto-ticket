What is Free vs What Requires Access

✓ 100% Free for Everyone No subscription needed

  • Train & Quota Selection: Automatic Tatkal / General quota & train choice.
  • Passenger Autofill: Auto-fills all passenger details, age, berths & food preferences.
  • Booking Review: Auto-navigates through review booking screen up to payment.

☕ Requires Buy Me a Coffee Access Optional convenience feature

Your data stays on this device.

Login, journey, passenger, and payment details are saved only in this browser on your local machine. They are not uploaded or stored on any remote server.

Uninstalling or removing the extension automatically deletes all saved booking data.
